Silent SOS: alert your loved ones without a sound

Sometimes you can't take out your phone or make a call. HearMe's silent SOS alerts your relatives without anyone around noticing: just say your secret phrase.

How it works

  1. 01

    Pick a secret phrase

    An ordinary phrase only you know, like “blue umbrella”.

  2. 02

    Link your relatives

    Up to 2 relatives on Telegram. SOS stays off until you turn it on.

  3. 03

    Say the phrase

    Your first name, location and the time go to your relatives, with no ringing.

What HearMe does

  • No ringing: the phone stays discreet.
  • With HearMe Plus, 2 new locations are sent 5 minutes apart.
  • An “I'm safe” button tells your relatives everything is fine.
  • The server keeps neither the message nor the location.

Frequently asked questions

Does SOS replace emergency services?

No. It alerts your relatives. If you're in danger, also call emergency numbers (112 in Europe).

Do my relatives need to install HearMe?

No. They get the messages on Telegram, without installing the app.

Is the secret phrase sent over the Internet?

No. It's recognised on your phone. Only your first name, location and the time are sent to your relatives.
