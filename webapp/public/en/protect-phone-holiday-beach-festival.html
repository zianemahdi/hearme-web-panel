Holidays, beach, festival: how to protect your phone

Updated November 16, 2026

On holiday you're relaxed, surrounded by people and far from home: that's when phones are most easily lost or stolen. And losing it abroad also means losing your tickets, your cards and your contacts.

At the beach, the pool, the hotel

  1. Don't leave it on the towel

    While you swim, a phone lying on a towel is the easiest target. Give it to someone or put it at the bottom of a closed bag.

  2. Use the room safe

    At the hotel or in a rental, leave what you don't need in the safe: passport, second phone, cards.

  3. Arm anti-theft before you walk away

    With HearMe, if someone takes or moves the phone, a loud alarm goes off and only stops when it's unlocked.

  4. Protect it from sand and water

    A waterproof pouch keeps it from breaking down at the worst moment, when you need it most.

At festivals, concerts, in crowds

  1. An inside pocket or in front of you

    In a crowd, avoid the back pocket and an open bag on your back. A closed pocket or a bag worn in front is safer.

  2. Agree on a meeting point

    If you lose your friends and the network is saturated, a place agreed in advance means you don't depend on the phone.

  3. Keep some battery

    A power bank and battery saver keep the phone reachable and findable for longer.

  4. A discreet SOS if something goes wrong

    With HearMe's silent SOS, saying your secret phrase sends your location to your loved ones on Telegram, without anyone noticing.

What if it gets taken anyway?

With HearMe anti-theft armed, you and up to 2 people get an alert on Telegram, with the location and a photo of the person at every wrong code. You can make it ring and lock it from gethearme.me, even from a hotel computer.

Before you leave, in 5 minutes

  • Note your IMEI (*#06#) and your carrier's number so you can block the SIM card from abroad.
  • Check that Find Hub (Find My Device) is on and that you know your Google password.
  • Set a lock code and back up your photos.
  • Install HearMe, link Telegram and add someone who stays reachable.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if my phone is stolen abroad?

Locate and lock it, have your carrier block the SIM card, change your passwords and report the theft to the local police with the IMEI. Follow our guide "Phone stolen: what to do?".

Does the alarm go off if I pick it up myself?

Yes if anti-theft is armed. Disarm it first, or unlock the phone to stop the alarm.

Does HearMe work abroad?

Yes. The keyword works even without internet. To receive alerts and the location, the phone needs a connection (mobile data or Wi-Fi).
