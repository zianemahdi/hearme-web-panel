Phone switched off or battery dead: how to find it?

Updated November 16, 2026

A phone that's off no longer sends its location: that's what makes the search hard. But all is not lost. Its last location is often known, some models stay findable for a few hours, and you can act now for the day it's switched back on.

If your phone is off or out of battery

  1. Check its last location

    On another phone or a computer, open Find Hub (google.com/android/find) with your Google account. If it no longer responds, its last known location is often shown.

  2. Pixel 8 or newer: check quickly

    On Pixel 8, 9 and 10, the Find Hub network can still locate it for several hours after it's switched off or the battery dies. Don't wait.

  3. Go back to the last known place

    If the location is a café, a station or a bus, ask the staff or the lost and found. Many "switched off" phones are simply out of battery and forgotten.

  4. Call it from time to time

    If it's switched on by someone honest, a call or a text may be enough to get it back.

If it was stolen and switched off

  1. Mark it as lost

    In Find Hub, choose "Mark as lost": the lock and your message will apply as soon as it reconnects.

  2. Block the SIM card

    Call your carrier to suspend the line, so no one can use your calls, texts or verification codes.

  3. Protect your accounts

    Change the passwords of your Google account, your email and your banking apps from another device.

  4. Report it with the IMEI

    Give the IMEI number to the police and your carrier: it lets them block the phone and identify it if it's found.

Do this today, in 2 minutes

  • Check that Find Hub (Find My Device) is on in Settings, Google.
  • Note your IMEI number by dialing *#06# and keep it outside the phone.

Frequently asked questions

Can you locate a phone that's switched off?

Usually not, because it no longer sends anything. You can see its last known location. On Pixel 8, 9 and 10, the Find Hub network can locate it for a few hours after it's switched off.

What happens when the phone is switched back on?

It reconnects and sends its location again. If you marked it as lost, it locks and shows your message.

Does HearMe stop the thief from switching the phone off?

No, that's not possible on Android without modifying the system. But HearMe sends a last alert with the location at the moment it's switched off.
