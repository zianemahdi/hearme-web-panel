Find a lost or stolen phone

You don't know where your phone is, or someone took it. From any computer or a relative's phone, sign in at gethearme.me with your HearMe account and take back control.

How it works

  1. 01

    Open gethearme.me

    Sign in with Google, your e-mail or your backup PIN.

  2. 02

    Switch to lost or stolen mode

    The phone sends its location, battery and network.

  3. 03

    Act remotely

    Ring it, lock it or take a photo of whoever has it.

What HearMe does

  • Live location on the map, with battery and network type.
  • Photos of the person taken on every failed unlock, shown in the panel.
  • A message and contact on the lock screen so an honest finder can give it back.
  • Commands also available by Telegram message, for you and your relatives.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install something before losing the phone?

Yes. HearMe must be installed and signed in to your account beforehand. After that, everything happens on gethearme.me.

Is the panel free?

Yes. The app and the gethearme.me panel are free.

How long are locations kept?

30 days at most, then they're deleted automatically. Only the last known location stays visible.
