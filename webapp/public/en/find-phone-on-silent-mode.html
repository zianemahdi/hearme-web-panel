Can't find your phone on silent? 8 ways to find it

Updated November 16, 2026

Your phone is somewhere in the house, on silent, and calling it does nothing. Don't panic: here are the methods that work, from the fastest to the simplest.

Make it ring, even on silent

  1. With your Google account

    On a computer or another phone, open google.com/android/find and choose “Play sound”. The phone rings loud for 5 minutes, even on silent. It must be switched on and connected to the internet.

  2. With a speaker or another Google device

    If you have a Google smart speaker or a tablet on the same account, say “Hey Google, find my phone”: it starts ringing.

  3. With your smartwatch

    Most smartwatches have a “Find my phone” feature that makes it ring while the watch and phone are connected over Bluetooth.

  4. On iPhone or Samsung

    iPhone: the Find My app on another Apple device or icloud.com/find, then “Play Sound”. Samsung: smartthingsfind.samsung.com, then “Ring”.

With no internet or other device

  1. Call it anyway

    On silent, many phones still vibrate. Call it from another phone in a quiet room and listen for the buzz against furniture.

  2. Retrace your steps

    Think of the last time you used it, then go back through the same places in order: sofa, bed, bathroom, kitchen, car.

  3. Search with a torch

    Turn off the lights and sweep under furniture and between cushions with a torch: the screen or case reflects the light.

  4. Check bags, pockets and the car

    Jacket pockets, bags and the gap between car seats are the most common hiding places.

Why don't these methods always work?

Remote ringing needs the internet and another device at hand. With HearMe, you just say your keyword out loud: the phone rings loud, even on silent and even offline, because the word is recognised on the phone itself.

Never lose it again: get ready in 2 minutes

  • Check that Find My Device is turned on in your Google settings.
  • Write down google.com/android/find so you have it at hand.
  • Pair your smartwatch with the phone if you have one.
  • Install HearMe and choose your keyword: say it out loud and the phone rings.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make a phone on silent ring?

Yes. Google's “Play sound” makes it ring even on silent, if it's on and connected. HearMe makes it ring when you say your keyword, even offline.

What if the battery is dead?

A phone that's switched off can't ring. Google's page usually shows the last known location. HearMe can also warn you when the battery is almost empty.

Can the HearMe keyword go off by itself?

You choose your own word. An option makes you say it twice, so a TV or another person can't trigger it by mistake.
