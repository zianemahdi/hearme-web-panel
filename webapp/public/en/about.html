About HearMe

HearMe was born from a situation everyone knows: the phone you can't find, on silent under a cushion, or the one you're afraid of having stolen in a café. Our goal is simple: keep your phone always within reach.

What HearMe does

Voice keywordSay your keyword: the phone rings loud, even on silent and offline.Anti-theft alarmIf someone takes your phone, an alarm goes off and you're warned.Find your phoneLive location, ring and remote lock from gethearme.me.Silent SOSA secret phrase alerts your loved ones on Telegram, without a sound.

Our commitments

  • No ads, no selling of data, no profiling.
  • Your keyword is recognised on the phone: HearMe never records or sends audio.
  • Your location is only sent when the phone is reported lost or stolen, in search mode, or when you ask to locate it.
  • Your data is hosted in the European Union (Paris) and sent encrypted.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time.
  • HearMe is for protecting your own phone: the app stays visible, with a notification while protection is on.
