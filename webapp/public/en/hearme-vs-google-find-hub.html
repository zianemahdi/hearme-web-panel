HearMe or Google Find Hub: which one should you use?

Updated November 16, 2026

Google already puts Find Hub (formerly Find My Device) on almost every Android phone. So what is HearMe for? Short answer: Google helps you find a lost phone, HearMe reacts the moment someone takes it. The two work well together.

What Google Find Hub does very well

  1. It's already there

    As soon as you add a Google account, Find Hub is on. Nothing to install.

  2. It rings for 5 minutes

    Even on silent or vibrate, the phone rings loudly for 5 minutes, from another phone or from the web.

  3. It can erase everything

    If you know you won't get it back, you can erase all your data remotely.

  4. It uses a large network

    Depending on the model, other Android devices help locate yours, even without a connection.

What HearMe adds

  1. An alarm at the moment of theft

    With anti-theft armed, if someone takes or moves the phone, a loud alarm goes off. The thief can't stop it without your code.

  2. Your voice is enough

    Phone lost at home or at the bottom of a bag? Say your keyword: it rings, even on silent, with no other device.

  3. Your loved ones warned right away

    Alerts, location and photos arrive on Telegram, for you and up to 2 people.

  4. A photo of the person

    At every wrong code, a photo is taken and sent to you. Useful for the police report.

Our advice: keep both

HearMe doesn't replace Find Hub: it works alongside it. Keep Find Hub on so you can erase the phone as a last resort, and add HearMe for the alarm, the keyword and the alerts to your loved ones.

Turn them on in 2 minutes

  • Open Settings, Google, then Find Hub (or Find My Device) and check that it's on.
  • Set a lock code or a fingerprint on your phone.
  • Install HearMe, choose your keyword and link Telegram.
  • Arm anti-theft with one tap whenever you put your phone down in public.

Frequently asked questions

Does HearMe replace Find My Device?

No. They work together. Find Hub is for locating and erasing remotely; HearMe adds the anti-theft alarm, the voice keyword, the photo and the alerts to your loved ones.

Is Google Find Hub free?

Yes, it comes with Android and your Google account. HearMe is free too.

Which one rings a phone on silent?

Both. With Find Hub you need another device or a computer. With HearMe you can also simply say your keyword.
