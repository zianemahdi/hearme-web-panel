Make your phone ring on silent, just with your voice

Your phone slipped under a cushion, it's on silent, and nobody is around to call it. With HearMe, just say your keyword out loud: the phone rings loud and you find it.

How it works

  1. 01

    Pick your keyword

    A word or short phrase of your own, like “where is my phone”.

  2. 02

    Turn on listening

    A notification stays visible while HearMe listens. You can stop it at any time.

  3. 03

    Say it out loud

    The phone rings loud, even on silent. Say your stop word to end it.

What HearMe does

  • Recognition happens on the phone itself, even without Internet.
  • “Say the keyword twice” option so a TV or another person can't trigger it.
  • Adjustable sensitivity for your voice and your room.
  • HearMe does not record or send audio: it only looks for your keyword.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work when the phone is on silent?

Yes. That's the point: HearMe makes the phone ring even on silent.

Do I need an Internet connection?

No. The keyword is recognised on the phone with an offline speech model.

Does HearMe listen to my conversations?

No. The phone only looks for your keyword. Conversations are never recorded or sent.
