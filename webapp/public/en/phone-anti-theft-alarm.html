How it works

  1. 01

    Arm the anti-theft

    One tap in the app. A notification shows that protection is on.

  2. 02

    Someone grabs the phone

    HearMe detects the movement and a loud alarm goes off.

  3. 03

    You get alerted

    Alert on Telegram (and by e-mail if you turn it on), with the phone's location.

What HearMe does

  • To stop the alarm, the phone must be unlocked: a thief can't silence it.
  • A photo of the person on every failed unlock, sent to your Telegram and your panel.
  • Automatic lock if stolen (with device administrator permission).
  • Alerts when the battery is almost empty or the phone is switched off.

Frequently asked questions

Does the alarm go off if I move the phone myself?

Yes, if anti-theft is armed. Disarm it before picking your phone up, or unlock it to stop the alarm.

Can my relatives be alerted?

Yes. You can link up to 2 relatives on Telegram: they get the alerts, photos and location.

Is the anti-theft free?

Yes, the anti-theft alarm is part of the free app.
