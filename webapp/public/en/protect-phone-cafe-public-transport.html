Phone theft in cafés and on public transport: 8 ways to avoid it

Updated November 16, 2026

Most phone thefts take a few seconds, in busy places: café terraces, stations, the metro, buses. A few simple habits are enough not to be an easy target.

In cafés, restaurants and libraries

  1. Don't leave it at the edge of the table

    A phone on the street side or the walkway side can be taken by someone passing by, often hidden under a menu or a newspaper. Keep it close to you, on the wall side.

  2. Watch out for distractions

    Someone asking you the time, showing you a piece of paper or spilling something can be there to turn your eyes away while someone else takes the phone.

  3. Never leave it alone

    Even for a minute to order or go to the toilet: take it with you, or arm an anti-theft alarm before you step away.

  4. Not in a back pocket or an open bag

    Use a front or inside pocket, or a closed bag worn in front of you.

On the metro, bus and train

  1. Put it away near the doors

    The most common snatch happens just before the doors close: the thief grabs the phone and steps out. Near the doors, keep it put away.

  2. Hold it firmly in a crowd

    In a packed carriage or bus, a firm grip on the phone makes it much harder to take.

  3. Earphones in, eyes on the screen

    With music in your ears and your eyes on the screen, you notice less of what's going on around you. Look up at each stop.

  4. Don't fall asleep holding your phone

    On long journeys, put it in a closed pocket before you rest.

Frequently asked questions

Where is phone theft most common?

In busy places: café terraces, stations, the metro, buses, festivals and concert exits. The risky moments are when you're distracted or in a hurry.

Does the alarm go off if I pick up my own phone?

Yes, if the anti-theft is armed. Disarm it before picking it up, or unlock the phone to stop the alarm.

What should I do if my phone is stolen?

Don't chase the thief, then follow our guide “Phone stolen: what to do”: locate it, lock it, block the SIM card, report it to the police.
